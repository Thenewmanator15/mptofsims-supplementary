Driver mismatch null

The gate needs its two driver channels to make identical pulses. They don't: one pulse comes out wider than the other. That mismatch shrinks as the drive voltage rises, so at some voltage it would reach zero. That voltage is the null. The question on this page is whether the driver can reach it.
