Supplementary material: interactive figures

Interactive versions of figures from the PhD thesis on multiplexed time-of-flight SIMS. The thesis PDF is the examined record and carries a static version of each; these pages add what print cannot. Every page states whether its data are measured or illustrative.

  • Beam-Current Averaging
    Scrub the averaging window over measured picoammeter baselines, 16 noise-survey conditions, and watch coherent interference integrate away faster than the square-root-of-N white-noise law.
  • BNG Die Topography
    Interactive height map of a Bradbury-Nielsen gate die, measured on a Keyence VHX-5000 by depth-from-focus.
  • BNG S-Parameters
    Measured S-parameters of three fabricated Bradbury-Nielsen gates, 30 kHz to 4 GHz at full 10,001-point resolution: zoom the resonances, switch parameters, view on a Smith chart.
  • Capture Sample Rate
    Drag the digitiser's sample rate and phase over 152 measured single-ion pulses and see how much of the peak a 1 GS/s grid misses.
  • Clock Reference Jitter
    Measured jitter and Allan deviation of the instrument's 100 MHz clock reference: choose the integration band and see what the oscilloscope floor allows the measurement to say.
  • BNG Driver Edges
    Measured output edges of the BNG gate driver across a 19-200 ns symbol-width sweep, and why its common-mode rejection falls with rate: channel matching, not bandwidth.
  • C60 Impact MD
    Molecular-dynamics simulations of single 20 keV C60 impacts on the gate metals: sputtered atoms against time, by crystal face, angle, energy and electronic stopping, against the binary-collision lower bound.
  • Multiplexed Acquisition
    Simulation on measured BioTOF parameters: when does Hadamard-multiplexed acquisition beat pulsed, and what does excess noise cost? Move the spectral occupancy and compare noise models.
