Interactive figures

Figures from the thesis Efficient High Speed Modulation Techniques for a Sensing Application (multiplexed time-of-flight SIMS), with the data behind them. Each page lets you explore what a printed figure can only show one slice of, and says what it shows, where its data came from and what to check before quoting a number. The thesis PDF remains the examined record.
